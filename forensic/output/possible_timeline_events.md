## Methodology

Tags are applied by keyword matching and forensic-linguistic pattern recognition. The approach draws from:

- **Evan Stark (2007)** *Coercive Control* — patterns of isolation, monitoring, micro-management
- **M. Johnson (2008)** *A Typology of Domestic Violence* — distinguishing incident-based from pattern-based violence
- **Duluth Power & Control Wheel** — eight coercive tactic categories
- **LIWC (Linguistic Inquiry & Word Count)** — psycholinguistic word categories
- **Home Office DASH Risk Indicator Checklist** (SafeLives, 2009)
- **CPS guidance on Controlling or Coercive Behaviour** (2015, updated 2023)
- **DARVO** (Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender — Freyd 1997)
